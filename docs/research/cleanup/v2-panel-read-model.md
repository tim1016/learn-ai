# Clean-up findings — v2 panel read model: bot page, gallery, lane summary, deploy

- **Ticket:** [Clean-up — v2 panel read model](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/issues/3151), a module ticket of [Map — Robustness and cleanliness](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/issues/3146).
- **Read at:** `origin/master` `862b50d3` (2026-10-13 21:17 CDT). No open PR touched this scope; the sibling tickets #3149, #3150 and #3152 were running on disjoint files.
- **Scope:** 48 Python app files (`app/services/broker_v2_panel/` except `option_*`, `app/broker/v2panel/`, the two routers) and 61 test files (`tests/broker/v2panel/` minus the `option_*` tests, three under `tests/services/`, the gallery tests under `tests/routers/` and `tests/schemas/`). Seven schema modules that serve only this surface were searched too. Frontend files are named only where they decide whether a Python route or field is live.
- **Depth:** all seven lenses.
- **Cut PR:** [#3167](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/pull/3167)

## Verdict

**One cut PR, one P1 filed, no P0.** The cut removes the journal-derived half of the panel projection (it ran on an empty journal and the SQLite adapter overwrote every output), three unreachable readers and a never-raised exception class: **−1,367 / +114 lines** in 15 files, in-scope suite 1,381 → 1,364 passed (the 17 are the tests whose subject went), no skips, no behavior change (independent differential, below).

**P1, filed at once:** [#3164](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/issues/3164) — the bot page header prints "IBKR market data live · updated …" for any running bot, whatever the feed is doing (F1).

Beyond those: 49 further findings (F2–F50), all P2, robustness or hygiene. The closest to P1 are F5 (the served transaction rail never says "blocked"; a failed or canceled order with no fill reads "Waiting — nothing is wrong") and F2 (a Dry Run admission *preview* creates and registers a simulated authority and leaves it behind; reproduced). Twelve carry an owner-ruling disposition: F7, F8, F19, F20 (budget-less), F26, F28, F32, F36, F37, F39 and two inside F44.

What this module does well, so the next reader does not redo it: every route in the two routers has a Frontend service method with a component caller; every closed-vocabulary member has a producer or is retired ledger-read widening; no `except Exception: pass`, no `print`, no TODO without an issue; money arithmetic is `Decimal`/integer cents; unknown is `None`, not zero, in the lane counts, budget statement and open P&L; the idempotency ordering and the archive re-proof are deliberate and tested.
