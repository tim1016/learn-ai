- **Scope:** `app/broker/fleet/` (23 files, 13,004 lines), `app/broker/contract/` (8, 2,018), `app/broker/capture/` (3, 528), their three test directories (60 files, 25,292 lines), the fleet routers (`internal_fleet.py`, `fleet_*.py`), the broker routers (`broker.py`, `brokers.py`, `broker_dependencies.py`, `broker_bots.py`, `broker_clerks.py`, `alpaca_clerk_sqlite.py`, `alpaca_live_graduation.py`, `clerk_transactions.py`) and their router tests, and the fleet operation catalogs and OpenAPI contract as they relate to these routes.

## Verdict

**Two P1s filed, no P0, and twelve small pieces of dead machinery cut in one delete-only PR, [#3170](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/pull/3170)** (22 files, +14 −237, Python only; no change to OpenAPI, the fleet operation catalogs or the generated TypeScript types). One independent review returned FAIL on a test deletion that was not the duplicate it was read as; that deletion and a second, behaviour-changing-in-a-double-fault cut were reverted, and the scoped re-review passed.

| Filed | What | Why P1 |
|---|---|---|
| [#3168](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/issues/3168) | `create_registry_backup` reads the manifest's lane list *after* it writes the snapshot, so a drain-and-release that commits in between leaves a lane that is `effective` in the snapshot but missing from the manifest. After a restore that lane is never asked to reconcile, the hold clears without it, and it routes. Reproduced end to end by the reader and again by the driver. | A gate fails open. The window is milliseconds in a manual host-CLI ceremony; the owner may rate it P2. |
| [#3169](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/issues/3169) | `BrokerOrderLeg.quantity` accepts `Infinity`. It passes preview, reaches the broker port (the submit answered 500 *after* the port was called once), and leaves a row the account projection cannot read, so the account's Clerk status and custody diagnosis answer 503 until the row is repaired. Reproduced by the reader and again by the driver. | The validation gate at the money-path edge fails open for one value. It needs the control secret and a non-UI client and fails closed afterwards; the owner may rate it P2. |

Nothing else in the module can place, duplicate or fail to cancel an order, or make custody differ from the broker. The routed-command path (pin before dispatch, exclusive dispatch claim, settle, never redeliver, cancelled dispatch stays a lost outcome) is covered by passing tests. The module is healthy where it was checked: the committed OpenAPI is in step with the code (`export_openapi_contract.py --check` exits 0), the fleet operation catalog has 97 operations and every one resolves to a mounted route, 12 of the 13 operations the [#2706 kill list](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/issues/2706) named are gone from the catalog, both snapshots and the export (the thirteenth, `custody_runs_stop`, stays by the owner's 2026-09-30 ruling), and no ban-list time item, silent handler or `print` turned up in the app files.

The findings that matter most after the two P1s, all `fix` for the handoff:

- **A/F1** one unparseable stored lane summary makes `GET /api/broker-clerks` answer 500 for every lane (reproduced). The resilient twin was cut in #2755 and the runbook has no 500 row.
- **B2/F2** the lane-quiet gate never refuses a negative confirmation age: after a backward clock step an expired confirmation reads as quiet (reproduced).
- **B1/F1, D2a/F1** the public stream's 15 s keepalive cancels the generator, so an idle stream emits one `: keepalive` and then ends (reproduced through the real chain). Self-corrects through the browser's reconnect.
- **B1/F2, D2a/F9** path parameters are spliced raw into the agent URL; `?`, `#` and `..` re-target the forwarded request. The agent's own fences are the second wall and the caller needs the control secret.
- **D1/F1** a non-ASCII `X-Fleet-Agent-Token` for a known clerk answers 500 instead of 403 (reproduced).
- **D2b/F1** `GET /api/accounts/{a}/transactions/{id}` answers 500 for a foreign account while its siblings answer 422 and 404 (reproduced).
- **D1/F2** `_ADAPTER_VERSION` is still `alpaca-fleet.13` after 8 operations were added and 13 removed: the registration-time mixed-build refusal holds only if a person bumps it (owner ruling).
