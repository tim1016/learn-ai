# Options Golden Search: evidence and verdict contract

Investigation date: 2026-10-20. Read-only code inspection plus the local API captures supplied by the parent investigation. No production code, study record, bot or database was changed.

## Findings

### The linked study cannot be the search that selected the cited combined setup

The study `os_d7b4752086147e437a3a0a56ecc980f33d3c6055` names `SPY PUT 25mom no stop 0945-1545 baseline 2026-10-18`. Its frozen protocol summary has one put, capital $10,000, and searches only `legs.put_atm.momentum.value` in 20, 25, 30. Run 76 has a call with 35% momentum and a put with 50% momentum, a 35% stop on each, no re-entry, and $10,000 capital. The latter is outside this search's dimensions. The study has no final results or qualification ID. This is an identity/lineage mismatch, not proof that the profitable combined strategy failed this search.

Evidence: The investigation captured local API records `study` and `run76`; the PRD records the relevant identities, periods and values. The full local API dump is intentionally not published as a portable fixture. Names are labels; exact setup hashes and recorded links are the authority.

### The study stopped on minute-risk evidence after four sessions

The study baseline has four daily observations, two traded sessions, net -$339.60 and 2 missing marks out of 599 required held marks. All three displayed candidates are `could_not_evaluate`. Two missing-mark instants are `1730925480000` and `1730925600000`. The declared development period contains 373 scheduled sessions; none of six forward folds produced an eligible winner. These are partial diagnostics, not an assessment of the full development history, and do not describe run 76's 499 sessions.

This behavior is deliberate in current implementation: [evaluator.py:93](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/app/research/options_golden_search/evaluator.py#L93) stops before the next session if the current outcome is incomplete, the underlying is missing, any held minute lacks a contemporaneous mark, or capital evidence ends. [test_evaluator.py:77](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/tests/research/options_golden_search/test_evaluator.py#L77) explicitly tests that a profitable, closed session with a missing intermediate mark stops the following day too.

Run 76 reports $17,109.10 net over 499 sessions, 465 traded sessions, 573 leg trades and no incomplete sessions. Its result is descriptive simulated performance under its setup and fill assumptions. This alone is not evidence of successful parameter selection or untouched confirmation.

### Ordinary replay and Search intentionally answer different risk questions

Both use the same session replay. Its optional observer reads the actual post-fill holdings and contemporaneous bars without changing trading decisions ([replay.py:181](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/app/options_backtest/replay.py#L181), [replay.py:246](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/app/options_backtest/replay.py#L246)). A missing bar makes observed equity unavailable ([observation.py:64](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/app/options_backtest/observation.py#L64)).

Ordinary runs carry each session's P&L, including the diagnostic convention of marked but unclosed positions; a separate capital verdict records whether that cash was actually feasible ([run.py:30](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/app/options_backtest/run.py#L30), [run.py:170](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/app/options_backtest/run.py#L170), ADR 0078 decision 10). The dashboard derives session-close risk and cannot reconstruct minute risk ([dashboard.py:15](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/app/options_backtest/dashboard.py#L15)). Search uses maximum minute-close loss from its running high divided by fixed starting capital, and recovery at minute observations ([metrics.py:179](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/app/research/options_golden_search/metrics.py#L179)). A similar-looking drawdown percentage on these pages is not necessarily the same measure.

Qualification requires every frozen rule. Technical invalidity dominates performance failure, then sparse evidence, then pass ([policy.py:52](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/app/research/options_golden_search/policy.py#L52), [policy.py:86](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/app/research/options_golden_search/policy.py#L86)). Ranking applies only among eligible candidates ([search.py:132](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/app/research/options_golden_search/search.py#L132)). A high net result cannot override unknown risk.

### Missing minute bars need diagnosis, not automatic backfill

ADR 0078 explicitly states that a minute with no trade has no bar and fills cannot be interpolated or carried forward ([ADR 0078:55](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/docs/architecture/adrs/0078-options-backtest-clock-driven-replay-of-bought-legs.md#L55)). The vendor also explains that no eligible trade can produce no aggregate bar. A blank minute can therefore be normal market activity, not a corrupt download. [Massive's official explanation](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/why-are-there-missing-aggregates-in-massives-data).

For these exact two gaps, the capture proves missing contemporaneous marks; it does not prove whether the reason was no eligible trade, ingestion loss or source-file change. A correct diagnostic names the contract, time, retained file identity and source status before offering remediation. Downloading the same data cannot be promised to create a trade that never occurred.

### Golden approval is not synonymous with successful Golden Search

Ordinary Golden approval checks the complete source run and independent replay proof, then records its setup and performance; it does not record a search study or require the search's statistical gates ([golden.py:199](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/app/options_bots/golden.py#L199)). Its explicit meaning is owner-approved for the exact tested setup, not automatically searched ([golden.py:47](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/app/options_bots/golden.py#L47)).

Search approval additionally retains a manifest tying the frozen protocol, all trials/evaluations, final comparison, exposure, failures and owner exceptions to the exact approved point ([approval.py:168](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/app/research/options_golden_search/approval.py#L168)). Search approval materializes and proves an ordinary run on the final window, not an arbitrary whole-history run ([approval.py:136](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/app/research/options_golden_search/approval.py#L136)). It requires exact session outcomes and inputs to agree with the scored final evaluation ([approval.py:151](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/app/research/options_golden_search/approval.py#L151)).

The existing `ready` status means approval plus current execution proof. It is not a prediction of profitability. Search-derived approvals separately go stale when their qualification identity changes ([golden.py:100](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/app/options_bots/golden.py#L100)). Bots use exact Golden IDs and setup hashes ([runtime.py:126](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/0fc274728c42b443755afc37db8d16314b0dfe82/PythonDataService/app/options_bots/runtime.py#L126)). The UI should expose these existing distinctions.

## Proposed evidence contract

1. **Exact identity and provenance.** Every page carries a human-readable strategy summary, full stable-leg settings, canonical setup hash and source kind: ordinary backtest approval, search approval, manual derivative, or unrelated. Join study → candidate → source run → Golden version → bot using recorded IDs and hashes. Similar names or overlapping settings may suggest a comparison but must never fabricate ancestry.
2. **Typed scope on every measure.** A metric includes value, units, basis, evaluation window, requested sessions, observed sessions, traded sessions, source identity and status: complete observation, partial diagnostic, unavailable, or modeled estimate. Missing is null with a reason, never zero. Decimal money and int64-ms time remain authoritative.
3. **Separate verdict dimensions.** Show descriptive profitability, evidence completeness, frozen research-rule eligibility, comparison to original, historical exposure, replay-proof validity and deployment readiness separately. A concise server-authored lead verdict composes those facts; do not invent a confidence percentage or opaque composite score.
4. **Structured search history.** Expose frozen ranges, objective, eligibility rules, search method and its limitations, complete candidate/evaluation counts, invalid/rejected counts, selected hashes, fold winners and reasons no winner was selected. Do not call 27 evaluations 27 distinct strategies.
5. **Typed diagnostic causes.** Failure codes should identify first affected session, minute, leg, contract, missing-data class, truncated coverage and allowed next action. Present “4 of 373 sessions replayed; minute risk unavailable” before Sharpe or ranking from those four days.
6. **Candidate evidence on demand.** A read-only endpoint restores retained exact evidence for any admitted candidate hash and scope, with pagination and bounded curves. It never reruns or fetches replacement bytes. Failed candidates remain inspectable.
7. **Chart metadata.** Curves preserve null/gap segments, actual observed boundaries, drawdown extrema, sample counts and sampling method. Separate session-close P&L from minute valuation. A calendar cell distinguishes no trade, skipped, incomplete, not evaluated and measured zero.
8. **Paired comparison.** Baseline and contender comparisons align exact common sessions and list every setup/model difference. Different periods remain separate. Forward folds describe the selection procedure; a synthetic pooled path is never called one candidate's backtest or compounded funded equity (ADR 0080).
9. **Approval audit.** Keep source type, selected candidate count, exposure status, exceptions, proof version and operator note visible in approval and bot views. Show “owner-approved backtest; no linked search qualification” when that is what exists.

## Missing-mark policy proposal

Preserve current immutable study verdicts. In a new explicitly versioned protocol, consider allowing descriptive replay to continue after a session whose real fills fully close every position, whose cash reconciles and whose capital evidence remains valid, even if an intermediate valuation mark is missing. Record that risk coverage failure permanently; minute maximum drawdown and recovery remain unavailable or explicitly partial, and qualification under the current strict minute-risk policy remains blocked. Continue to stop on unclosed positions, unsupported cash settlement, invalid capital or genuinely missing required input artifacts.

This separates “we cannot measure every minute's risk” from “we cannot replay later cash flows” without weakening a gate or inventing prices. It requires explicit implementation and regression proof, not a relabel of the current negative four-session result. Existing evaluations/IDs must not be mutated; new execution/selection identities and linked studies are required.

If a usable alternative risk standard is desired, record a new owner decision and frozen protocol: for example, clearly scoped session-close research with independently reported minute coverage, or a documented quote/valuation source with its own model assumptions and parity evidence. That is a changed scientific claim. Do not silently forward-fill, use future prices, erase gaps, call observed-only drawdown a verified maximum, or substitute it into an unchanged 20% minute-risk gate. Market-data provider and execution-provider boundaries remain unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria for implementation

- Opening this exact study immediately says PUT-only 20/25/30 search, 4/373 replayed sessions with incomplete minute risk, missing minute marks, no eligible forward winners and final not opened. It never displays the combined CALL35/PUT50 run as a selected descendant.
- Opening run 76 says +$17,109.10 over 499 sessions under simulated fills and costs, with its exact two-leg rules. Its approval lineage is independently resolved and labeled.
- A comparison of these two objects lists differing legs, momentum, stops, dates, metric basis and coverage before showing profit differences.
- A rejected candidate opens retained charts and trades; an unavailable trace explains why. All plots preserve unknown intervals and stop at the observed end.
- “Completed” is disambiguated as job complete versus research evidence sufficient. Technical invalidity cannot be owner-waived. Final not opened differs from final failed.
- Every new number is computed in Python using an identified canonical function, provenance and tolerance-pinned tests. Frontend transforms only display geometry and formatting.
- Closed-session/intermediate-gap tests prove continued descriptive replay cannot use synthetic cash. Separate tests preserve stop behavior for unclosed positions and invalid capital. Same setup, inputs, window and fill assumptions must reconcile to the ordinary backtest's session outcomes.
- Multi-candidate final selection and previously exposed dates remain plainly disclosed. A new full-history run cannot retroactively turn already-inspected dates into an untouched confirmation set.
- Full approved manifests and sources remain immutable and undeletable. Re-proof cannot silently upgrade an old statistical verdict. Deployment remains a separate action.
