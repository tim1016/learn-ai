Ticket: [Retail execution correction protocol and evidence limits](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/issues/2434). Map: [Codex research map](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/issues/2413). Source baseline: **`10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43`**. Public official documentation checked 2026-09-24. Research used an isolated clone and the research skill; no provider/account/market-data endpoint or operational state was accessed.

**Result: no new Critical/High or other material defect established in this bounded protocol pass.** Public retail Trading API documentation establishes ordinary fill observation and paginated activity queries, but does not establish a complete correction/bust acquisition, supersession or replay contract. The explicit correction protocol found belongs to the separate Broker API. The active repository refuses unknown event names and records degraded stream health; this is not proof that all economic corrections are recoverable. **71 offline adapter, consumer and historical-recovery controls passed.** The earlier contradictory-aggregate finding is not re-counted or withdrawn.

The repository pins [alpaca-py 0.42.0](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/requirements-light.txt#L38-L49). That version's official [TradeUpdate model](https://github.com/alpacahq/alpaca-py/blob/v0.42.0/alpaca/trading/models.py#L587-L600) accepts `event` as an enum **or string** and makes `execution_id` optional. Therefore the SDK model is not proof of an exhaustive immutable event vocabulary or a complete correction protocol. The active repository intentionally owns raw capture and stricter mapping rather than delegating custody interpretation to that model.

## Active behavior and its limits

**Proven from source: unknown named events are refused before economic folding.** The [adapter vocabulary and parser](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/adapter.py#L373-L461) raises on an unrecognized event and requires execution identity for recognized fill events. Neither `trade_bust` nor `trade_correct` is supported. The [consumer captures bytes before parsing](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/trade_updates.py#L509-L542), and its [mapping failure path](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/trade_updates.py#L568-L598) increments parse errors, marks evidence unhealthy and logs the problem. This is explicit degradation, not silent conversion of an unknown correction into an ordinary fill.

**Proven/tested: degraded health is temporary stream evidence, not a correction-recovery receipt.** [Health projection](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/clerk/stream_health.py#L74-L128) names an unusable evidence frame, and [the active ENTER boundary](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/clerk/sqlite/runtime.py#L984-L1012) refuses unhealthy stream evidence. A later valid durable event, or an already durable duplicate, [restores health](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/trade_updates.py#L620-L681). Existing tests exercised that recovery. Thus the refusal must not be described as a permanent unresolved-correction hold.

The [stated rationale](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/trade_updates.py#L45-L52) is that quiet accounts and a lack of custody heartbeat make silence an unreliable health test; “A later successfully mapped live trade-update frame restores health.” This pass does not recommend reversing that stream-health decision without a demonstrated supported retail correction input. It does identify the limit of the claim: usable recent transport evidence and resolved economic lineage are different facts. If vendor clarification confirms an economic correction can take this currently unknown-event path, evaluate a durable order-level conflict separately from transient channel health.

**Proven/tested: reconnect recovery is bounded aggregate recovery.** The [consumer's gap routine](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/trade_updates.py#L698-L738) first invokes authoritative reconciliation, then requests a bounded page of closed orders. Its comment says the stream has no cursor replay; the narrower externally supported statement is that no replay cursor is exposed by the reviewed public retail contract. The [SQLite sink](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/clerk/trade_evidence.py#L196-L225) labels recovered aggregates separately and does not invent exact execution IDs. This is a satisfied provenance boundary, not complete replay of every lost execution or correction.

**Proven/tested: activity recovery is a narrow paper-account operation.** [Recovery admission](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/clerk/sqlite/historical_execution_recovery.py#L131-L204) requires the expected paper account, one matching execution, correct order and symbol, valid side/positive economics and a source timestamp. [Preparation](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/clerk/sqlite/historical_execution_recovery.py#L324-L360) refuses unavailable history or economics that do not match and rechecks the authority revision. This is not a live-account or universal correction/bust importer. The [read loop](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/broker.py#L156-L195) is explicitly bounded to [three pages](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/broker.py#L38), and a missing match remains a refusal.

**Identity bridge needs a narrower claim than a provider guarantee.** The [historical bridge](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/clerk/sqlite/historical_execution_recovery.py#L206-L218) strips a numeric `timestamp::` prefix to compare an activity suffix with a retained execution ID. The retail guide shows that activity-ID shape, but the reviewed public pages do not explicitly guarantee that the suffix always equals the websocket execution UUID. Additional order, symbol and economic checks constrain this bridge; no safe reproduction demonstrated a wrongly accepted supported identity. Vendor confirmation remains warranted before calling the relationship a universal contract.

**No second finding from the prior aggregate discrepancy.** A correction appearing only as a changed cumulative price remains the independently established issue from [Economic execution records and correction authority](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/issues/2428). This protocol research neither establishes its real-provider frequency nor supplies a guarantee that makes it impossible. No source was found promising immutable aggregate price, correction delivery before aggregate updates, or indefinite replay availability.

## Questions that require vendor evidence

1. For retail Trading API US-equity executions, how are a venue price/quantity correction and a complete bust delivered? Request the actual event/activity schema, ordering and original/superseding execution identity rules, including paper/live differences.
2. May a filled order's cumulative quantity or average price change without a separate exact correction event? Which observation is authoritative, and can the event arrive after the aggregate?
3. Is there a supported retail cursor/retention/replay mechanism for all economic corrections, including those created after the original trade date? Are historical activity rows append-only or revisable?
4. Is the activity-ID suffix guaranteed to equal the retail websocket execution ID, and is that identity immutable and unique for the account across corrections, reconnects and history reads?

These are factual protocol questions, not owner preferences; no vendor message was sent and no owner answer was invented. Until answered, retain explicit bounded-recovery language, unknown-event degradation and economic conflict handling. A future change should be justified by the retail contract actually obtained, without borrowing Broker API or market-data semantics. No fixes or PR were produced.
