## Findings

### A1 — A cash snapshot can release reservations for fills it never included

- **Claim:** The account envelope timestamps a cash snapshot after a parallel positions request completes, allowing another bot to spend cash already consumed by a fill recorded during that wait.
- **Goal:** Correctness; architecture; trading intelligence.
- **Severity:** **Critical** — silently wrong new-exposure admission under the hard cash-only risk promise. Actual broker execution/borrowing is conditional on the broker account and is not observed here.
- **Evidence — reproduced:** `research/codex-2415/test_account_risk_repro.py::test_cash_read_must_cover_fills_before_reservations_are_released`. Two registered active instances share $1,000. The first reserves 10 × $100. The synthetic account request returns $1,000; an exact $1,000 fill is then recorded while the positions coroutine remains pending. The later envelope timestamp changes reserved cash from $1,000 to $0 and a second instance's $1,000 ENTER is accepted. The expected refusal fails with `DID NOT RAISE AdmissionBlockedError`.
- **Evidence — proven:** [live_envelope_sync.py:258–280](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/clerk/sqlite/live_envelope_sync.py#L258-L280) gathers independent account/position reads, then stamps the combined observation. [envelope_reservations.py:79–105](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/clerk/sqlite/envelope_reservations.py#L79-L105) treats every effective fill recorded before that timestamp as already covered by cash. [envelope_admission.py:64–84](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/clerk/sqlite/envelope_admission.py#L64-L84) uses that reservation total. [runtime.py:1026–1066](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/clerk/sqlite/runtime.py#L1026-L1066) rechecks market liveness but does not establish a cash-snapshot execution watermark.
- **Why it matters:** Freshness and transactional acceptance do not prove that an earlier broker balance includes later executions. This is especially reachable with concurrent bots and asymmetric read latency. The first same-instance reproduction was correctly refused as `ENTER_IN_PROGRESS`; changing to a second instance reproduced the account-level problem. Intake serialization cannot make the earlier broker response include the later fill. The race also overstates cash after a delayed account response; it does not require stale clocks, duplicate callbacks, or failed persistence.
- **Recommendation:** Give broker cash observations an explicit conservative coverage boundary and reconcile reservations against that boundary. Do not infer covered executions from the completion time of unrelated reads. Preserve reservations through ambiguous cash/execution overlap, and prove the cross-bot admission invariant under deterministic interleavings.
- **Confidence:** High on the local unsafe acceptance, reproduced through the real repository/admission code. Broker cash refresh semantics and actual margin permission affect downstream consequence, not the demonstrated local breach. A verified stronger composition fence that prevents the interleaving would change the conclusion; none exists in the inspected independent envelope-sync path.

### A2 — Unbounded market entries contradict the hard cash-only guarantee

- **Claim:** Regular-session market DAY orders are admitted at the decision bar's close, although their eventual execution cost is unbounded by that price.
- **Goal:** Best execution; correctness; trading intelligence.
- **Severity:** **High** — realistic adverse execution can exceed the advertised risk limit; an account may reject or borrow depending on broker constraints. No real borrowing was tested.
- **Evidence — reproduced:** `test_cash_only_acceptance_requires_a_bounded_execution_price` admits 10 shares with $1,000 cash at a $100 reference, reserves exactly $1,000, folds a synthetic valid $101 market execution, and reads $1,010 cash spent from the actual economic projection. The invariant assertion fails `1010.0 <= 1000.0`.
- **Evidence — proven:** [program_leg.py:381–389](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/clerk/program_leg.py#L381-L389) explicitly creates MARKET/DAY with `limit_price=None`. [runtime.py:1056–1066](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/clerk/sqlite/runtime.py#L1056-L1066) passes the retained close to [envelope_admission.py:70–83](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/clerk/sqlite/envelope_admission.py#L70-L83). Reservations price post-observation fills using the original reference, not actual execution price ([envelope_reservations.py:99–105](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/clerk/sqlite/envelope_reservations.py#L99-L105)).
- **Decision rationale challenged:** [ADR 0059:66](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/docs/architecture/adrs/0059-real-money-live-behind-shadow-gate-arming-and-cash-bound-envelope.md#L66) says the cash bound makes “an Intraday Margin Deficit … impossible by construction.” Its [Decision 5.3](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/docs/architecture/adrs/0059-real-money-live-behind-shadow-gate-arming-and-cash-bound-envelope.md#L83) preserves regular-session market DAY. The rationale for leaving that order type is continuity with existing program behavior and the extended-hours broker restriction. That continuity does not establish a maximum regular-session cash debit, so these two accepted promises cannot both hold. This does not advocate per-order caps or reverse the owner's provider choice.
- **Why it matters:** At full available cash even small adverse price movement can exceed the boundary; several bots can under-reserve actual filled cost until the next cash read. The bound is an estimate while the product/ADR asserts an invariant.
- **Recommendation:** Preserve cash-only intent with execution-price-bounded entries and conservative all-in cash reservations, or explicitly replace the hard guarantee with a separately approved estimated-notional policy. Reserve actual filled cost until broker cash coverage is established. A quote or arbitrary slippage buffer alone is not a hard bound on a market order.
- **Confidence:** High on the mismatch and synthetic economics. The exact production incidence depends on sizing and market movement. A demonstrated broker-side cash-only execution constraint covering all supported account types would reduce practical risk, but is not the local guarantee claimed.

### A3 — The daily loss gate can miss a losing day on a carried position

- **Claim:** `day_pnl` combines today's realized P&L with total unrealized P&L since entry, so earlier accumulated gains can conceal today's loss.
- **Goal:** Correctness; trading intelligence.
- **Severity:** **High** — the account-wide daily loss policy produces the wrong day result for ordinary overnight holdings. This is a design-definition defect, not an implementation deviation from its ADR.
- **Evidence — reproduced:** `test_daily_loss_must_measure_change_from_prior_close` retains 100 shares bought at $100 before today, with prior close $200 and current mark $150. Cash is unchanged at $90,000, prior equity $110,000, current equity $105,000. The actual daily loss is $5,000 and the configured dollar loss limit is $5,000. The envelope reports a known, complete `day_pnl=+5000`, publishes a fresh cash observation, and does not regard the account as breached. Assertion expecting `-5000` fails against `+5000`.
- **Evidence — proven:** [day_pnl.py:50–79](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/clerk/sqlite/day_pnl.py#L50-L79) restricts realized FIFO to today's window but adds the unrebased unrealized total. [live_envelope_sync.py:272–280](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/clerk/sqlite/live_envelope_sync.py#L272-L280) obtains total position unrealized; [same file:298–324](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/PythonDataService/app/broker/alpaca/clerk/sqlite/live_envelope_sync.py#L298-L324) judges and publishes that reading.
- **Decision rationale challenged:** [ADR 0059:29](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/docs/architecture/adrs/0059-real-money-live-behind-shadow-gate-arming-and-cash-bound-envelope.md#L29) states “A day-P&L fact can be composed without new math” from realized-today plus broker unrealized. [Decision 4:67](https://github.com/tim1016/learn-ai/blob/10b5f31b529c8507bd19bb24015f3d85fa9aba43/docs/architecture/adrs/0059-real-money-live-behind-shadow-gate-arming-and-cash-bound-envelope.md#L67) adopts it as the daily loss hold. Reusing correct FIFO and correct total-unrealized components does not make their time bases compatible: the no-new-math rationale fails specifically when positions cross the day boundary. The reverse case can also block trading because of an earlier-day loss despite recovery today.
- **Why it matters:** Profit carried into today provides unintended headroom against today's loss cap. Closing a carried position also recognizes its whole lifetime profit/loss in today's realized component rather than today's change from the prior close. A flat-at-every-boundary strategy avoids this, but the Accounts product and risk envelope do not enforce that restriction.
- **Recommendation:** Decide the loss budget's exact time basis, then implement a reconciled account equity-change or daily-mark accounting authority with cash-flow/fee/corporate-action treatment and boundary evidence. If the intended policy is the current hybrid measure, name it honestly and add a distinct daily-loss control rather than claiming it is daily P&L.
- **Confidence:** High on the demonstrated metric and policy mismatch. An owner decision that expressly wants this hybrid lifetime/session measure would change the policy recommendation, but not the arithmetic or naming issue. That choice remains open.

Trace `exit_resolution`, `recovery_reduction`, and `exit_watchdog`; prove with synthetic interleavings. Existing targeted tests provide positive starting evidence, not a blanket conclusion.

4. **Owner decision:** Must the cash-only envelope be a hard execution-cost guarantee (recommended, consistent with ADR 0059), or can it be an estimated bar-price notional check? A hard guarantee requires price-bounded execution or an independently proven broker-side constraint, not just a larger heuristic buffer.
