# ADR 0084: Navigation is place-first: six groups, and an instrument, a signal strategy and an options setup are each a place

Date: 2026-10-23

**Status:** Accepted 2026-10-23

**Vocabulary:** `CONTEXT.md` § "Places (resolved 2026-10-23)" — **instrument place**, **signal strategy**, **options setup**, **Operations**. "Account workspace" keeps the meaning ADR 0064 gave it.

Extends ADR 0064 (Alpaca navigation is account-first). Contexts: PRD #3490 (§2, R01, R31, R32, T01) and slice #3497.

## Decision

1. **Six groups, in this order:** Markets, Research, Signal strategies, Options setups, Accounts, Operations. A group is a trigger and never navigates; its first page is its landing. There is no Documentation group.
2. **A place is where the owner works, and it keeps its identity across the tabs inside it.** ADR 0064 made the account one. An **instrument** (one symbol), a **signal strategy** (one registry key) and an **options setup** (one saved name) are places too. Each is addressed by its URL alone, highlights its group on every tab and detail page, and fails in place, with a way back, when the thing named does not exist. Nothing opens a default instrument, strategy or setup instead.
3. **Options setups are separated from signal strategies by strategy model, not by traded asset.** An options setup is a saved, timed, rule-based setup replayed by Options Backtest and approved through Golden Options. A registered signal program that trades options stays a signal strategy, with its own proof, sealed settings and Golden Search.
4. **Help replaces the Documentation menu.** The top-bar "?" lists every served document (`HELP_DOCUMENTS`), Architecture Manual and Legal Notices included, and a page's "How this works" link opens the one that explains it. A document belongs to no group, so opening one lights none up, and its window title is its own name.
5. **Titles.** A list page's title is its menu label, and the window reads `Page · Group`. An entity page is titled with the entity, its tab and group in the breadcrumb and window (`SPY · Chart · Markets`). A page's breadcrumb leads back to its group and to the list it sits inside.
6. **A lost address fails in place.** An address no page answers opens a not-found page at that address, with a way back to Markets. It is never redirected onto a default page, which drops the query and fragment and hides that the link was wrong.
7. **A move is one redirect.** When a slice moves a page, the old address goes straight to the final one, keeping query and fragment. A redirect that depends on stored evidence (a run, study or version id) resolves its owner from that record, or fails in place keeping the native id.

## Canonical addresses

Later slices build these and redirect old addresses to them. Nothing below exists yet, and no page has moved. Dates and instants in a query are `int64 ms UTC`, except the date-picker form values ADR 0075 allows.

| Place | Address |
|---|---|
| Markets | `/markets` (held instruments and search; `?then=options` or another tab asks which instrument first) |
| Instrument | `/markets/:symbol/(chart\|returns\|options\|data)`; all headlines `/markets/:symbol/chart/news`; dataset builder `/markets/:symbol/data/build`; options views `/markets/:symbol/options/(chain\|surface\|pricing)` with `?source=(snapshot\|live\|archive)&expiry=&date=&at=` (`date` and `at` for the archive only; see the 2026-10-24 amendment) |
| Signal strategies | `/signal-strategies` (list); comparison `/signal-strategies/recency`; one strategy `/signal-strategies/:key/(overview\|define\|explore\|golden\|proof)`; Explore `/explore/(backtest\|sweep\|walk-forward)` with the native `?run=` or `?search=`; Golden `/golden/(configuration\|studies\|versions)` and `/golden/studies/:studyId`; evidence whose owner must be resolved `/signal-strategies/evidence/(run\|search\|study)/:id` |
| Options setups | `/options-setups` (list); one setup `/options-setups/:name/(overview\|define\|explore\|golden)`; immutable evidence `/options-setups/evidence/(run\|study\|golden)/:id`; the analytical builder `/options-setups/analysis` |
| Research | `/research`; one study `/research/(feature\|cross-sectional\|signal\|indicator-reliability\|realized-vs-iv\|rule-sandbox)`; demonstrations `/research/demos/(cross-asset\|regimes\|edge-score\|trade-simulator)`; History `/research/history` (`?type=&symbol=&status=&page=`) and `/research/history/(feature-experiment\|signal-report\|spec-run\|walk-forward\|monte-carlo\|baseline)/:id` |
| Operations | `/operations/(lake\|option-archive\|backups\|math-checks\|data-checks)`; data checks `/operations/data-checks/(tradingview\|divergence\|pre-flight)` |
| Accounts, documents | Unchanged: `/brokers/alpaca/…`, `/docs/…`, `/legal/notices` and the other document addresses |

Two rules keep these unambiguous without reserving names. A setup or strategy address always carries its tab, so a bare second segment never names one, and `evidence`, `analysis` and `recency` are never taken for a name. Evidence addresses have three segments after the group, which no tab address has.

## Why

- The owner starts from a thing (a symbol, a strategy, a saved setup, a study), and the old menu started from a tool. Each tool asked which thing again, and one page carried two names (the Stocks menu entry over the Data Lab heading).
- An address that names its place is what lets Back, Forward and reload keep the context, and what lets a link to a run or a version reach the page that owns it.
- Splitting by strategy model keeps two histories, two proofs and two execution paths apart even where they trade the same instruments.
- The Documentation group put thirteen documents in the main menu and tied each to a menu rather than to the page it explains.

## Considered options

- **Keep tool-first groups and rename them.** Rejected: it leaves the repeated "which symbol, which strategy" question.
- **Split signal strategies from options setups by traded asset.** Rejected: a signal program that trades options would move out of the registry that proves and seals it, and a timed options setup has no signal program to prove.
- **Keep Documentation as a seventh group.** Rejected: the help index already lists every document, and a seventh trigger costs menu width.

## Consequences

- Pages keep their present addresses in this slice. The menu file lists the pages that still sit in another place's shell (`pages` on a group), and later slices delete those entries as the pages move.
- The route-inventory spec (T01) holds one row per declared route and the group it must light up. A route added without a row fails it.
- Legal Notices has no footer link today; it stays reachable from the help index. Whether to add a footer is an owner decision.

## Amendment 2026-10-23: the instrument's context rides in its address (#3501)

The instrument place (`/markets/:symbol/chart`, and `/markets/:symbol/chart/news` for every headline) is built.

- **Context keys.** The window, bar timeframe and session are query keys, `?from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD&timeframe=(1m|5m|15m|30m|1h|4h|1d)&session=(regular|extended)`, and every tab carries them. The two dates are the date picker's form values (ADR 0075; ADR 0022 (f) names the deviation): a host builds `int64 ms UTC` from them at its own request (Data Lab's anchors, UTC midnight to the end of the end date), and nowhere else.
- **Defaults are shown.** A missing timeframe or session reads as `1d` and `regular`. An address with no window opens on the 1Y range Python resolves, shown in the picker and written to the address only when something is committed over it. A window that is half there, unreal or reversed is refused in place with its reason; nothing is substituted for it.
- **Commit rules.** A shared picker commits by moving the address; there is no Apply and no Refresh. A half-typed, refused or reversed entry commits nothing. A commit equal to what is committed does nothing. A distinct, complete context loads once, a request a newer one replaced is ended, and its answer, even one already delivered, cannot fill the newer context.
- **Opening an instrument.** A held instrument opens. A listed one the lake does not hold goes through the one gate of ADR 0066 and opens when the lake confirms the bars (landing on its address is the pick); leaving releases only this place's hold on the fill. An address that names no symbol the lake can hold fails in place, with the way back to Markets.
- **Old addresses.** `/data-lab` and `/data-lab/explore` lead to the instrument their `ticker` names, with its window and `trading-session` (now `session`), or to Markets when they name none. `/research-lab/market/news` leads to that instrument's headlines the same way. `/markets?then=<tab>` opens the tab it names for the instrument picked, where that tab is routed, and Chart where it is not.

## Amendment 2026-10-24: each header fact says where it came from (#3502)

- **One read.** `GET /api/markets/instrument-facts?symbols=` (at most 25) answers an instrument's header and one page of the Markets list. Python computes every figure; the browser formats and computes none.
- **The price names its basis and its as-of time.** It is the freshest source that answers: a live quote from a line the data plane already streams (the read never opens one), else the vendor's delayed snapshot (one request for the batch, used only when it observed a trade after the last completed close), else the last completed close from the lake. The canonical calendar decides which session that is. IBKR stays the read-only live feed, the data plane may hold no Gateway session, and no Alpaca market data is read. Each quote lists the sources it passed over, with why.
- **The day move names its close.** It measures from the close of the last completed session dated before the price's own ET day, so an after-hours price is measured from the day before, not from the close it follows. Closes come from the split-adjusted lake tree the place opens, so the header's held span and its close are the same bars.
- **The latest price is not a chart price.** The facts are read for the instrument alone. No window, timeframe or tab asks again or changes them, and a price from inside a window is never shown as the latest.
- **A failed source costs only its own fields.** The price and move say "unavailable" with each source's reason, the archive badge says "unavailable", and the place still opens.
- **Span and archive wording.** The held span is the lake's first and last held day, labelled a span. The archive badge says "some days stored" or "every required day stored" over the archive's own span, and never claims coverage of a window; whether a requested window is covered is the Options tab's question.
- **Lists are paged.** Markets draws 20 rows and asks for those rows once; each "Show more" is one request for the rows it adds. No row opens a subscription or a fill (T22).

## Amendment 2026-10-24: Build dataset and saved setups live under the instrument (#3504)

The Data Lab's dataset builder and its saved setups are the instrument place's. Nothing under `/data-lab` is a page of its own any more: every address there is a redirect.

- **Build dataset** is `/markets/:symbol/data/build`: a secondary action in the instrument's header on every tab, and a page under Data, which stays the open tab. It reads the workspace the place already holds: the instrument, window, bar timeframe and session the address commits, the forward-fill and split-adjustment policy, and the indicator recipe the Chart tab holds. Companion options, output formats, the plan's provenance, progress, cancel and download are the builder's as before. Opening the page asks Python for a plan and starts no job; only its Generate button does. The run dock sits in the place's shell, so a job started here stays in view on the other tabs, and its saved expanded/collapsed choice keeps its key, `run-dock-expanded:data-lab`.
- **Saved setups** are the records `/api/data-lab/sessions` holds, unchanged. A header action opens the drawer (list, save, update, rename, delete). Opening a setup reads that one record, gives the workspace its recipe, scope and saved chart through `DataLabWorkspaceStore.restore` (schema v2, the validator that names what it drops), and moves the address to the setup's instrument, window, timeframe and session, so the setup opens under the instrument it is for. Its saved chart becomes the answer the Chart tab already holds, so no chart request is made; a chart whose timeframe the place does not offer, or that lacks a part the chart draws from, is not drawn, and the page says so. Whatever the page could not take is named in a "Saved setup" notice and stays in the record: opening never writes. "Update setup" replaces only the setup of the instrument the place is on.
- **`?sessionId=<id>`** on an instrument address asks for the same opening. The id is spent on arrival: the address that results carries the setup's own window, so Back, Forward and a reload never open it twice. The record names the instrument, so an address that claims another is moved to the record's, never rendered under the wrong header.
- **Old addresses.** `/data-lab`, `/data-lab/explore`, `/data-lab/export` and `/data-lab/returns` lead to the instrument their `ticker` names (Chart, Chart, Build dataset, Returns), with its window and `trading-session` (now `session`) and every other key kept, or to Markets when they name none. `/data-lab/validate`, and a `mode` that names Validate, lead to Operations → Data checks → TradingView comparison with their scope; an archive-card anchor (`#option-archive-…`) leads to the Operations tab that holds that part of the card, query and fragment intact. A key the Chart, Returns and Data tabs do not read is named in the place's "Migrated link" notice. A `sessionId` with no `ticker` reaches Markets, which does not read it: a redirect cannot read the record that names the instrument.

## Amendment 2026-10-24: the Options tab's chain and its four modes (#3505)

The instrument's **Options** tab is built: `/markets/:symbol/options` opens its Chain view, and `/markets/:symbol/options/(chain|surface|pricing)` are the three views, declared once (`OPTIONS_VIEWS`). A view the tab does not route yet stays in the strip, greyed with its reason, and leaves `UNAVAILABLE_OPTIONS_VIEWS` when its route is added.

- **The tab's own context.** The chain's address keys are `?source=(snapshot|live|archive)`, `expiry` (the option expiry anchor, 16:00 New York time on its date, `int64 ms UTC`), and for the past `date` (the session date, the date picker's `YYYY-MM-DD` form value, ADR 0075 and ADR 0022 (f); the archive host builds New York midnight from it at its own request) and `at` (the instant within that session, `int64 ms UTC`; absent reads the session's close). None is a key of the instrument context, so moving an option date or instant never moves the chart's window, and Chart still has its window on return. A link between the tab's views carries the instrument context and the expiry, and no other key of the chain. Over the Options tab the instrument bar offers only the instrument, and a window the chart cannot use stops nothing there.
- **One presentation, typed source adapters.** Every source maps into one chain presentation, so the grid can show only what a source's type can carry. A contract's fields are a complete record, each filled or absent with a reason; a last-trade price is the `last` field and never a `bid` or `ask`; a Greek, an open interest or a volume appears only from a source that holds one, and the archive holds none, so each is absent for it and no current value stands in for a past one.
- **The four modes.** *Now · Snapshot* is the provider's current snapshot, with its source, the time it arrived, and the provider's own delayed or real-time label; a refresh that failed reads as stale, with the reason. *Past · Archive* reads what the lake's archive held on a session date, at an instant of it, for an expiry: the three are separate controls, nothing observed after the instant is drawn (a value stamped later is an absent field with that reason), and each row shows the minute its price is the close of or why it has none (not stored, not traded by then, unreadable, or an expiry the date does not list). *Now · Live IBKR* is the retained read-only IBKR feed (see the #3506 amendment below). *Past · Live IBKR* does not exist: IBKR streams the present only, so its button is disabled with that reason, and an address that asks for `source=live` with a date or instant says so in place and never shows today's chain under a past date.
- **Nothing is chosen for the operator.** An address that names an unusable source, expiry, date or instant says what and loads nothing. A Snapshot opened without an expiry opens the nearest the provider lists, and writes it into the address (replacing the entry, so Back leaves the page) so the contract survives a mode change; an Archive opens no expiry and reads nothing until a date is picked. An expiry the source does not list is said, not replaced. Choosing a mode keeps the expiry and puts away the date and instant a Snapshot cannot have. Only the chosen source's adapter is mounted, so leaving a mode ends its requests and an answer still in flight cannot reach the next chain.
- **Add leg** stays disabled with its reason (#3494).
- **Old addresses.** `/options-lab`, `/options-lab/chain` and `/broker/options-chain` go to the Chain view in one navigation, keeping the instrument a `?ticker=` or `?symbol=` names, the expiry, the source, the date, the instant, every other key and the fragment. With no instrument they go to `/markets?then=options`, which asks for one; none is guessed. The Options Backtest page's Past chain tab and Build dataset's embedded Past chain are gone; the options companion links to the Archive mode for its ticker and the last day of its window. The Markets menu no longer lists Options Lab or Options Chain (Live); the Options Lab shell stays a page of the group for the Strategy finder stub.

## Amendment 2026-10-24: Now · Live IBKR and the cross-check (#3506)

*Now · Live IBKR* is built, and the Chain (Live) page it came from is gone.

- **One feed, one subscription.** The mode reads the retained read-only IBKR connection through the data plane's shared feed owner (ADR 0082), the only live provider: when the data plane holds no Gateway session no other provider stands in, and the mode says Live is unavailable with the reason the data plane gave, reads nothing from IBKR and opens no stream. The connection is the one app-wide `BrokerHealthService`; the chain page holds one subscription through its one `LiveChainFeed`, and no component under it opens a stream of its own. Three lines say the feed's state: the **connection** (the data plane's own condition words), the **entitlement** (IBKR does not report it on this feed, so the line says what is known: quotes are asked for in real time only and never fall back to delayed ones, how many contracts have sent a quote, and IBKR's latest message to the data plane when that message is a refusal) and the **subscription** (the strikes, contracts and market-data lines held, or why it stopped).
- **Nothing starts by itself.** Entering the mode, picking an expiry or picking strikes make read-only requests (expirations, qualifiable strikes) and subscribe to nothing; Start subscribes, once, to the strikes picked. A subscription is of one underlying, one expiry and one set of strikes. Changing the underlying or the expiry, pausing, losing the data plane's session or leaving the mode closes the stream, and the data plane releases every line the stream held (the shared feed owner cancels a line when no other consumer holds it). A frame is drawn only if it comes from the stream now held and names its underlying and expiry, and an expiry listing, strike list or engine reply that belongs to a chain since left is dropped when it arrives. A snapshot is current only while the stream is open and has delivered within five seconds; a paused or silent stream keeps its last snapshot on screen, said to be stale.
- **What the cross-check compares.** The columns compare **delta** per contract: IBKR's against the data plane's QuantLib engine's, as `|Δ IBKR − Δ engine|` in delta units, green up to 0.005, yellow up to 0.02, red above (the Chain (Live) page's calculation and tolerances, unchanged). The engine is valued with the contract's strike and right, its own IBKR implied volatility, the underlying's price as IBKR gave it when the snapshot was cut, the expiry at 16:00 New York time, the data plane's current New York date and default risk-free rate, and a dividend yield of 0. The page prints this above the table, with the instants each side is as of (each contract's quote; the snapshot clock for the underlying).
- **Unavailable is never zero.** A contract is not compared, and its cells read *unavailable* with the reason, when the stream is not delivering; when IBKR sent no delta, no usable volatility, no two-sided quote or no underlying price; when its quote is more than five seconds older than the snapshot (the data plane's own limit for a quote a decision may use); or when it expires on the snapshot's New York date, because the engine counts whole days and returns the intrinsic delta at none, which an intraday model delta cannot be held to. The chain also says how many contracts were compared and why the others were not.
- **Old addresses.** `/broker/options-chain` already carried `source=live` to this mode with the instrument and expiry; it needs no further entry.

## Amendment 2026-10-24: Surface and Pricing are views of the Options tab (#3507)

Surface (3D) and Pricing Lab are `/markets/:symbol/options/surface` and `/markets/:symbol/options/pricing`; with Chain, every view of `OPTIONS_VIEWS` is routed and `UNAVAILABLE_OPTIONS_VIEWS` is empty.

- **The instrument is the underlying.** Neither view asks for a symbol. Each keeps its own inputs (days out, strikes band and metric; rate, range and models) and its own provenance. Pricing opens on the expiry the address names and says so when the chain does not list it. Moving to another instrument keeps the view and follows the instrument.
- **The live views say what the data plane makes of the IBKR feed.** The Live IBKR chain and Surface each show the scoped-health notice for the `live_chain` operation, above their controls; whatever refuses an action keeps its own reason at that action.
- **Old addresses.** `/broker/options-surface` and `/pricing-lab` go to their view in one navigation, keeping the instrument a `?ticker=` or `?symbol=` names, the expiry, every other key and the fragment; with no instrument they go to `/markets?then=options`. The Markets menu lists neither.
