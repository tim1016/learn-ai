# ADR 0059 Slice 7 — Gate re-meaning, the live authority, and the first real order: design

**Decision record:** [ADR 0059](../../architecture/adrs/0059-real-money-live-behind-shadow-gate-arming-and-cash-bound-envelope.md) Decision 1 (a `real_live` authority exists only on three-way mode agreement — settings, a live activation record for the exact account id, the broker-observed mode — checked where an authority is constructed), Decision 3's last sentence (per-instance arming is what permits real-money submission), Decision 8 (a transition out of `live-armed` mid-session halts new submission with a `LIVE_VERDICT_TRANSITION_HALT` receipt; Resume is guarded), Decision 10 (fault injection and developer reset refuse live), Decision 11 (the thirteen gates change meaning, not count), Consequence 7 ("Gate re-meaning, schema widening, contract regeneration. This is the slice after which a real order is possible"). Vocabulary: `CONTEXT.md` § "Live account, shadow, and risk envelope". Predecessors whose evidence this slice finally *consumes*: slice 4 (`docs/references/alpaca-shadow-authority.md` — the receipt), slice 5 (`docs/references/alpaca-live-envelope.md` — the gate), slice 6 (`docs/references/alpaca-live-arming.md` — the record and the per-instance status rule).

The owner was away when this slice was designed (2026-09-09). Where the ADR decides, it decides; every choice below that the ADR does not make is a **controller ruling**, recorded with its cost if wrong so the owner can reverse it. Three rulings shaped operations rather than code and were put to the owner: **R1** (graduation is a boot-time selection, so a live account is shadowed *or* live-custodied, never both at once), **R10** (Decision 8's `desired_state = PAUSED` is deliberately **not** written, because PAUSED is observe-only for EXIT too and would strand a real position; the halt is the ENTER refusal, loudly) and **R14** (a live boot refuses unauthenticated data-plane control, which the operator's current `.env` sets). **The owner confirmed all three on 2026-09-09** (grill-me interview, relayed by the parallel session "Live trading PRD progress"), answered the eight review questions under Residuals, and added two requirements: ADR 0059 is amended in this PR (R10, R12) and a mid-session mode disagreement surfaces in the verdict under its own code (R2). **A second owner decision the same day: "Shadow should be just a mode. It should not be a requirement to run a bot."** So the arming ceremony no longer requires a shadow receipt (R7), graduation no longer requires one (R3), and Decisions 2 and 3's receipt sentences join the ADR amendment (R16). Shadow rehearsal remains available and its receipt is recorded when it exists. An independent pre-flight review of this design's first draft (`.superpowers/sdd/2026-09-09-live-slice-7-gate-re-meaning/survey-review-of-design.md`, 2026-09-09) is folded in below.

## Scope

**In:** the `real_live` custody world and its boot story (`live_authority.py`); the live activation through the existing cutover ceremony, widened; per-instance arming consulted at ENTER admission on the live authority, fail-closed on an unreadable ledger; arming consulted at Start/Resume admission; every one of Decision 11's gates re-meant exactly as the ADR lists them; the `live` execution mode on the deploy wire, the schema widenings that follow, and the OpenAPI/codegen regeneration in the same change; the live verdict widened past the shadow authority with copy that stops saying "nothing submitted yet"; the `LIVE_VERDICT_TRANSITION_HALT` reaction; the closed panel copy map gaining the codes that now reach a receipt; a `live-evidence:` retained-bar namespace; docs.

**Out (later slices, or follow-ups named under Residuals):** the live qualification campaign (slice 8); manual live orders (Decision 11: `manual_order_runtime` stays paper-only); short selling; running the shadow authority *and* the live authority on one account at the same time (R1's residual — a secondary-authority seam, follow-up); reversing a graduation (R1's residual); the paginated order-history walk; per-instance shadow progress on the verdict; the overnight session. No SQLite schema change. One change to the arming ledger's record shape: `shadow_receipt_sha256` becomes optional (`null` when the instance holds no receipt), `schema_version` stays 1 because no live arming ledger exists anywhere yet (owner decision 2026-09-09; see R7). No new environment variable.

## Rulings

- **R1 — Graduation is a boot-time selection, and one primary authority stands.** On a live-mode boot `select_active_clerk_runtime` asks the account cutover's `ActivationStore` (`accounts/alpaca/`) for a record naming the *exact* observed account id. Present ⇒ `select_live_clerk_runtime` composes the `real_live` authority as the primary. Absent ⇒ `select_shadow_clerk_runtime`, exactly as today. The repo's one-primary-authority rule (ADR 0035/0042, `set_active_clerk_runtime`) is kept: a live account is either shadowed or live-custodied by a given process, never both. *Cost if wrong:* after graduation a **new** instance cannot rehearse on that account until a secondary-authority seam exists (a live-custodied process installs no shadow authority), and graduation has no reversal in this slice (`dev_reset` refuses live by Decision 10; a `LiveDeactivationRecord` mirroring disarm is the obvious follow-up). For slice 8's small funded account — shadow every campaign instance, then graduate — this is acceptable; the owner can reverse by ordering the seam first.
- **R2 — Three-way agreement is re-stated at selection, not assumed.** `select_live_clerk_runtime` refuses `LIVE_MODE_DISAGREEMENT` unless all three hold at once: `settings.is_live`, the broker-observed `account.account_mode == "live"` (the adapter already refuses a shape disagreement, ADR 0059 D1), and an activation record whose `account_id` equals the observed id. The activation leg is a *lookup by the observed id* — a record can never name a different account into custody. Mid-session, the envelope sync's 15 s `get_account()` is the only reader that can see the broker's mode move; a `BrokerAccountModeDisagreement` there withdraws the envelope (slice 5) **and, on the live authority, invalidates the arming gate under `LIVE_MODE_DISAGREEMENT`**, so every ENTER refuses with that code — not `LIVE_ENVELOPE_UNOBSERVED` — until a read agrees again; logged once per transition; **and the live verdict reads the gate's fault and reports `mode_agreement = "disagreed"` with `clerk_refusal_reason_code = LIVE_MODE_DISAGREEMENT` and `final_verdict = "unknown"` for as long as it stands** (owner decision 2026-09-09: today it reads as an unobserved envelope). *Cost if wrong:* none — this restates Decision 1 at the one place custody can begin, and names the one place it can be un-made mid-session.
- **R3 — The live activation is the paper cutover ceremony, widened.** `BrokerCutoverEvidence.account_mode` becomes `Literal["paper", "live"]`. The evidence file is hand-authored by the operator (`scripts/manage_alpaca_sqlite_clerk.py::_read_cutover_evidence`; no code path reads the mode from the broker), so `_normalize_broker_evidence` stops demanding paper and the CLI instead refuses an evidence file whose `account_mode` differs from the configured `ALPACA_MODE` — the one mode fact an offline ceremony has. No shadow receipt is consulted (owner decision 2026-09-09: shadow is a mode, not a requirement). The broker-observed leg of Decision 1 is not the ceremony's to prove: R2 proves it at every boot that consumes the record. Two facts of the existing ceremony are kept on purpose and stated: `_validate_cutover_broker_state` requires the account **flat and order-free** to graduate (an operator flattens the live account by hand before the cutover — the shadow authority never submitted, so any position is a human's); and all three entry points (`initialize`, `plan`, `apply`) demand legacy authority artifacts unless a developer reset moved a prior activation aside — a rule a never-legacy live account cannot satisfy, so **the live rule is that live evidence itself permits an empty legacy set**: `allow_empty = evidence.account_mode == "live"` at all three sites, for both `_legacy_artifact_evidence` and `_runner_roster_evidence`, with a test that a never-legacy live account plans and applies while a never-legacy paper account still refuses. (The first draft required a shadow receipt here; the owner ruled on 2026-09-09 that shadow is a mode, not a requirement.) Plan/apply, the confirmation window, the absence of a force mode and the activation record's shape are unchanged. *Cost if wrong:* an operator can activate a live account nobody has rehearsed on — which is the owner's stated intent; every order on it still needs an armed instance.
- **R4 — The `real_live` composition** (`app/broker/alpaca/clerk/live_authority.py::select_live_clerk_runtime`) mirrors the real-paper path with four differences: (a) it requires `live_envelope_values`, refusing `LIVE_ENVELOPE_MISSING` like the shadow path; (b) it binds the **real** trade port through `bind_real_alpaca_ports(..., account_mode="live")`, whose `authority_kind` becomes `real_live`; (c) it composes `LiveEnvelopeGate(values, custody_is_simulated=False)` — the broker's cash already reflects the Clerk's own fills — with the account's `LiveArmingLedger` and a new `ArmingGate` (R5); (d) it returns `ActiveClerkRuntime(authority_kind="sqlite", account_authority_kind="real_live")` with the real trade-updates evidence sink, exactly as paper has. **No new runtime kind:** the facade is the same `sqlite` Clerk over a real account, and what differs — the envelope, the arming gate, the mode — is carried by `account_mode` and the gates it holds, so `SQLITE_FACADE_AUTHORITIES`, the five surfaces that select through it, and the six `Literal["sqlite", …]` declarations stay as they are; the *account* kind `real_live` is what every read contract already declares. `SqliteAlpacaClerkFacade` refuses to construct as `sqlite` + `live` without both an envelope and an arming gate (`AccountAuthorityIdentityError`): a live facade with a gate missing is a composition bug, never a permissive default. Every failure returns a typed `unavailable` runtime; a live boot never aborts the data plane. *Cost if wrong:* none — composition.
- **R5 — Per-instance arming is a fourth admission at ENTER, between `require_admission` and the envelope.** `accept_enter` gains `arming: ArmingGate | None`; when present, `require_arming_admission(gate, strategy_instance_id=..., now_ms=repo.clock())` runs after the holds/uncertainties and **before** the envelope, so an unarmed instance never reserves cash. The gate (`app/broker/alpaca/clerk/live_arming_gate.py`) holds the last verified `ArmingSnapshot(observed_at_ms, records, seals, configured_envelope)` published by `LiveEnvelopeSync.tick()` — which already reads the ledger every 15 s and now reads the runner's sealed bindings beside it — and admission derives `arming_status(...)` from that snapshot at *its own* `now_ms`, so a lapse at the ET-date boundary is enforced at the instant, not at the next tick. Refusals, each an `AdmissionBlockedError` on `Capability.NEW_EXPOSURE`, in this order: the last refresh invalidated the gate → the gate's own code (`LIVE_ARMING_LEDGER_INVALID` for a ledger that will not verify — the slice-6 residual closed: an unreadable ledger is a refusal at admission, never an absent arming — or `LIVE_MODE_DISAGREEMENT` from R2); no snapshot, or one older than `OBSERVATION_MAX_AGE_MS` → `LIVE_ARMING_UNOBSERVED`; state `unarmed` → `LIVE_ARMING_REQUIRED`; `lapsed`/`disarmed` → the status's own code (`LIVE_ARMING_LAPSED`, `LIVE_ARMING_REVOKED`, `LIVE_ARMING_SEAL_CHANGED`, `LIVE_ARMING_FUTURE_DATED`, `LIVE_ENVELOPE_DISAGREEMENT`). EXIT is never subject to it (Decisions 3 and 4: a lapsed instance keeps managing its position). The gate is installed only on the `real_live` authority; the shadow authority keeps rehearsing unarmed instances, because the shadow gate precedes arming. Every arming refusal joins `TRANSIENT_ADMISSION_REASON_CODES` beside the envelope's: the runner retries on the next decision clock and the *reaction* to a lost arming is R10's pause, never a fatal halt from inside admission. Two facts the paper path never needed: the real-money ENTER is priced against the decision bar's close exactly as the shadow ENTER is (`reference_price`), because a market leg with no price is `LIVE_ENVELOPE_UNOBSERVED` — the trade path already hands the retained bar to every authority since slice 3, and a test pins that a `real_live` RTH ENTER carries one; and the facade under `real_live` still does not bind fills to the bar (`binds_decision_bar` stays false — real fills come from Alpaca). *Cost if wrong:* an instance armed a second ago waits at most one 15 s tick before its first ENTER admits; a sync that dies refuses every live ENTER within 45 s (fail closed, the envelope's own posture).
- **R6 — On the live authority a launch is admitted; submission is what arming gates.** Three facts fix the shape. Deploy is atomic — `BotStartAdmission.start` seals the binding to the custody snapshot in memory, evaluates, and only then persists and launches (`record_launch` has one production caller), so nothing about an instance exists for the arming ceremony to read until a launch has been admitted. A strategy instance is immutable per account — `sealed_account_id` is inside `bot_configuration_hash`, and `_ensure_instance` / `_ensure_sealed_program` refuse a re-deploy that changes it — so the instance that rehearsed as `shadow:<id>` cannot be the instance that trades on `<id>`; the live run is a **new instance id**. And PAUSED already means "keep evaluating, submit nothing" (`PAUSED_OBSERVE_ONLY`, ENTER and EXIT alike), so parking an unarmed launch in PAUSED would also park any position it later carries. Therefore: a Start or Resume on the live authority is **admitted RUNNING exactly as a paper launch is**, and every ENTER it makes refuses `LIVE_ARMING_REQUIRED` (R5) — as a rejected receipt the panel shows on every decision — until the operator arms it; Decision 11's "admits live only for an armed instance" is satisfied at the submission seam, which is the only seam that can reach money. `RunAdmissionFacts` gains `arming: ArmingAdmissionFact(state, reason_code, explanation, next_step, observed_at_ms) | None` with states `ARMED | NOT_ARMED | UNREADABLE` (`None` for Dry Run, paper and shadow), composed by a shared resolver (`app/services/live_arming_admission.py`) from a read-only `account_arming(...)` when the custody world is `real_live` and the mode is broker-contacting. `evaluate_run_admission` refuses **only** `UNREADABLE`, with `LIVE_ARMING_LEDGER_INVALID`, immediately after the ADR 0054 corpus gate — a launch under a ledger nobody can verify is refused, not parked; a `NOT_ARMED` fact rides the admitted decision's explanation, naming the ceremony as the next step (the corpus stamp's own pattern). No `launch_intent`, no `initial_desired_state`, no Continue guard: `continue_paused` has no admission today and gains none, because nothing in this slice pauses. Paper, shadow and Dry Run launches are byte-for-byte unchanged. *Cost if wrong:* if the owner reads Decision 11 as refusing the launch outright, the alternative is a registration-without-run primitive in the binding repository (about one task) or a paused launch with a guarded Continue (the first draft of this ruling); under every reading the ENTER gate (R5) is what stands between an instance and a real order.
- **R7 — Deploying on the live world, and how the rehearsal's proof reaches the new instance.** The deploy view on a `real_live` custody world offers exactly two execution modes, `dry_run` and `live` — the `live` card that has been `planned` since slice 1 becomes `available` there and nowhere else, and `paper` / `shadow` are never offered on it (a label that lies is the ADR 0011 failure mode, slice 4 R14). The request, receipt and `admissible_modes` literals gain `live`, `AlpacaPaperDeployStrategy._admissible_modes_invariants` admits `("dry_run", "live")` as the third selectable pair, `_RECEIPT_COPY` gains its row, and the view's `account_label` reads `Alpaca live · <id>`. A `live` deploy mints a new instance, seals it on the live account id, and launches it running (R6); its receipt says that every ENTER refuses until the instance is armed. The arming ceremony then reads that instance's live-sealed binding exactly as it reads a shadow-sealed one today (`custody_account_ids_for` already admits both ids). **Shadow is a mode, not a requirement (owner decision 2026-09-09):** the ceremony no longer refuses `LIVE_SHADOW_INCOMPLETE`. It still asks `ShadowReceiptStore.current` for the instance's own current receipt on this account and records that receipt's sha when one exists; otherwise `shadow_receipt_sha256` is `null` and the instance arms all the same. No twin-identity lookup is built: a rehearsal under another instance id is information an operator can read in the shadow receipt ledger, not a fact the arming record has to carry (ruled for simplicity; cost if wrong: one lookup function, later). `LiveArmingRecord.shadow_receipt_sha256: str | None`; `schema_version` stays 1 — no live arming ledger exists yet and every test fixture carries a sha, so a reader of either vintage verifies. `ALPACA_LIVE_SHADOW_SESSIONS` stays a required live setting: it is the receipt threshold for whoever uses shadow mode and it is inside the sealed envelope's hash — making it optional would change the envelope's shape for nothing (ruled; cost if wrong: an operator who never rehearses still sets a number).

- **R9 — The verdict widens past the shadow authority, and its copy stops lying by omission.** `observe_arming` and `observe_loss_hold` read on any `SQLITE_FACADE_AUTHORITIES` runtime under live settings (the live id is `live_account_id_for_shadow_account(selected)`, which is the identity on a real id). The copy table gains the world: on `real_live` with `armed ≥ 1` the headline is `LIVE account {account} — {instances} armed, real-money submission open` and the detail says that an armed instance's ENTER is submitted to Alpaca; on `real_live` with none armed, `LIVE account {account} — real-money authority installed, no instance armed` and every ENTER refuses. The shadow rows' detail stops saying "slice 7 is what opens submission" and "no path submits a real-money order in this slice" — sentences this slice makes false — and says instead that nothing is submitted **under the shadow authority**, and that graduation (the live cutover) is what installs the authority that submits. `clerk_authority` keeps answering `sqlite` — it names the Clerk's kind, and `configured_mode` plus an un-prefixed `observed_account_id` already say which world — so the verdict schema is unchanged. *Cost if wrong:* a Frontend reader that wants the world in one field reads two; recorded as a follow-up, not built.
- **R10 — The halt (Decision 8) is the refusal, said loudly; `desired_state = PAUSED` is deliberately not written.** ADR 0011 §5's IBKR implementation was deleted in #1813 (`app/broker/safety_verdict.py`, `BROKER_SAFETY_VERDICT_TRANSITION_HALT`), and the one pause primitive that remains — `BotTaskRegistry.pause` — clears the run gate so that `bot_trade_strategy` discards **every** intent as `PAUSED_OBSERVE_ONLY`, EXIT included. Writing PAUSED on a lapse would therefore leave a real position unmanaged until a human returned, which is the outcome Decision 4 forbids ("nothing is stranded"), and with `ALPACA_LIVE_ARMING_MAX_SESSIONS=1` in the operator's environment it would happen every morning. So the halt is built from what Decision 8 actually asks for: **new submission stops** — R5 refuses every ENTER of an instance that is not armed, and the refusal is a rejected receipt on each decision the panel shows; **it is loud** — the sync, which holds the previous tick's snapshot, logs `live_verdict_transition_halt` at warning level once per instance per transition out of `armed`, naming the account, the instance and the reason code, and the verdict's detail names the instance and its code (slice 6 R11); **and resumption is guarded** — by the arming ceremony itself (plan/apply under a confirmation window), after which the next tick admits, and by R6 for a new run. Mid-session, the only verdict input that can move is `armed_instance_count` (settings, the runtime and the boot-time mode agreement are fixed; the loss hold is a hold), so the per-instance transition *is* the account transition; the R2 mid-session disagreement is the one other input and it takes the same path (gate invalidated, every ENTER refused, logged once). EXITs keep running, so the program keeps managing its own position, and the operator's reduce-only actions stay available (R17). No registry seam, no pause, no Continue guard: nothing in this slice writes a desired state; operator Pause and Continue stay exactly as they are, on paper and on live. **Owner decision 2026-09-09: this ruling stands, and ADR 0059 is amended in this PR** — Decision 8's sentence becomes "new entries are refused with a `LIVE_VERDICT_TRANSITION_HALT` receipt and the verdict says why; exits keep running; re-arming restores submission", carried as an "Amended 2026-09-09" note in the form ADR 0039's doctrine prescribes. *Cost if wrong:* none now — the alternative (a paused instance a human must Continue) was put to the owner as E1 and declined.
- **R11 — Codes.** Five new codes, strings equal to their names, beside the model in `live_arming.py` and added to `ARMING_REASON_CODES`: `LIVE_ARMING_REQUIRED` (never armed), `LIVE_ARMING_UNOBSERVED` (the gate has no fresh snapshot), `LIVE_ARMING_LEDGER_INVALID` (the last refresh could not verify the ledger), the ADR's `LIVE_VERDICT_TRANSITION_HALT` (the pause reason in R10), and `LIVE_CONTROL_UNAUTHENTICATED` (R14, a selection refusal, defined beside the other startup refusals in `live_authority.py`). `LIVE_MODE_DISAGREEMENT`, `LIVE_ENVELOPE_MISSING` and `LIVE_SHADOW_INCOMPLETE` are reused from where they live. The closed panel vocabulary (`app/broker/v2panel/vocabulary.py`, its two snapshots, the Frontend fallback map and `test_vocabulary_snapshot.py`) covers phases, desired states, hold reasons, stations and actions — not admission reason codes, which travel as backend prose on receipts (`LIVE_ENVELOPE_CASH_EXCEEDED` reaches the panel that way today). No new code enters it: the pause writes a desired state the vocabulary already names, with a `reason` string the panel renders as prose. If the plan's pre-flight finds a surface that runs an arming code through `copy_for`, that code joins the map through the regenerator, and nothing else changes.
- **R12 — Contract and Frontend.** The deploy wire's three `Literal["paper", "dry_run", "shadow"]` declarations (`AlpacaPaperDeployRequest.execution_mode`, `admissible_modes`, the receipt) gain `live`; `AlpacaPaperExecutionMode.mode` already carries it and its card flips from `planned` to `available` only on a `real_live` world (`paper_deploy_service._execution_modes`, whose `BrokerExecutionMode` gains `live`); `StrategySpec.submit_mode` (`Literal["live_paper", "shadow"]`) is **not** widened: nothing writes it (spec fixtures only), `SealedBotProgram` does not carry it, and run identity is already separated by `sealed_account_id` inside `bot_configuration_hash` — the owner's answer to E8 (2026-09-09): leave it, and **fix ADR 0059 Decision 2's sentence "`submit_mode` is hashed into run identity" to name `sealed_account_id`** in this PR's amendment. `RunAdmissionFacts` gains the arming fact (R6); neither facts model is in the OpenAPI snapshot, so that changes no contract. `sqlite_clerk_compat` derives `authority_kind` from the primary's custody world (`projection_authority_kind`), so `ClerkStatus` and `CustodyDiagnosis` report `real_live` instead of the `"real_paper"` default. The OpenAPI snapshot is regenerated **once**, in the task that lands the last schema edit, and `export_openapi_contract.py --check` is green from then on; the Frontend generated types follow through `npm run codegen:check`. The Frontend has four places that would call a live world "paper", each fixed with one spec case: the deploy form derives `brokerMode` from whichever broker-contacting card is `available` (`paper` / `shadow` / `live`) with no `'paper'` fallback when the live card is offered, and `setExecutionMode` stops dropping `'live'`; the deploy drawer's `DEPLOY_WORLD_BY_ACCOUNT_MODE` stops mapping `live → 'shadow'` and takes the world from the view's available card; the Operator lens's `SQLITE_PROJECTION_AUTHORITIES` gains `'real_live'`; and `trader-lens`'s `isPaperExecution` is renamed `isBrokerExecution` (it means `mode === 'trade'`). On the backend, `panel_projection_service._execution_policy("trade")` stops saying "Paper execution." and words the sentence by the primary custody world. The banner needs no change (copy is backend prose).
- **R13 — A live instance retains its bars under `live-evidence:<strategy_instance_id>`**, a fifth reserved prefix beside `paper:` and `shadow-evidence:`, chosen by `evidence_account_id_for(custody_kind="real_live")`, and **read under the same rule it is written under**: the writer (`PrimaryAccountBindingAuthority.source_bars`) already takes its kind from `primary_custody_kind()`, while the reader (`run_replay_proof.ledger_account_id_for`) derives it from `authority_kind_for_account(binding.sealed_account_id)` with the paper default — so a live instance's replay proof would read an empty `paper:` ledger. The reader derives the kind the way the compat seam does (`projection_authority_kind(sealed_account_id, primary_custody_world())`), and a test pins that writer and reader name one namespace for a live binding. A live instance's retained-replay warmup then reads exactly what *it* retained, never its former paper or shadow self's ledger, and the name is honest. *Cost if wrong:* none — a path, and one predicate written once.
- **R14 — Decision 10, verified and extended by one setting.** Fault injection is already double-gated (`fault_injection.injection_permitted` requires the flag *and* `get_alpaca_settings().is_paper`, failing closed), so a live-mode boot admits none of it without a code change; a test pins that under live settings the seam refuses. The extension: a live-mode boot with `DATA_PLANE_ALLOW_UNAUTHENTICATED_CONTROL=true` installs **no Clerk**, returning a typed `unavailable` runtime `LIVE_CONTROL_UNAUTHENTICATED` whose recovery names the setting — the #2014 posture: degrade, surface, never crash-loop. *Cost if wrong:* the operator's current `PythonDataService/.env` sets the control escape hatch to `true`, so the first live boot after this slice refuses until it is `false` — which is the accident the refusal exists to catch. Flagged for the owner in the PR body.
- **R15 — After graduation, the rehearsal's bindings are foreign to the live authority, and that is a named posture, not a crash.** The shadow instances stay on disk sealed on `shadow:<id>`; the live authority custodies `<id>`. Boot recovery already touches an authority only for candidates the authority itself lists, so a foreign binding is repaired from its own lifecycle files and never stopped through the live Clerk; a test pins that a live boot with shadow-sealed bindings present completes its recovery sweep. Any Start, Resume or Continue on such an instance refuses `SEALED_ACCOUNT_MISMATCH` (gate 4, unchanged). The graduation recipe tells the operator to Stop every shadow instance before the cutover. *Cost if wrong:* the roster shows retired-in-spirit shadow instances until the operator retires them.
- **R17 — Operator reduce-only actions run on the live authority unchanged.** ADR 0045's `execute_safe_flatten` and ADR 0051's cohort flatten are EXIT-shaped (they only reduce attributed exposure), were never mode-gated, and are what closes a position whose instance can no longer ENTER; Decision 11's "manual live orders" are the manual order ticket (`manual_order_runtime`, arbitrary legs), which stays paper-only. A test pins that the flatten paths carry no mode or arming refusal. *Cost if wrong:* if the owner counts a flatten as a manual live order (question E4), it is one refusal in each of two modules.
- **R18 — Cold start on the live authority is the paper path's, not the shadow path's.** `verify_shadow_namespace_empty` proves the *shadow* instance namespace has never held a Clerk-minted order, which is right for an authority that never submits; applied to the live authority it would refuse every boot after the first real order. The live authority inherits the paper path's proof instead — the cutover's flat-and-order-free evidence at graduation (R3) and `recover()`'s reconciliation of open orders at every boot — and that is stated in the reference note. *Cost if wrong:* a stray Clerk-minted order on the live account before graduation is caught by the cutover's open-order check, not by a namespace scan.

## Data flow — from shadow to the first real order

1. **Shadow (slices 4–6, now optional):** `manage_alpaca_shadow activate` → deploy the instance in `shadow` mode → `ALPACA_LIVE_SHADOW_SESSIONS` clean, twin-reconciled sessions → `manage_alpaca_shadow receipt`. A rehearsal is the owner's choice, not a gate.
2. **Graduate (R3, R1):** `manage_alpaca_sqlite_clerk cutover-initialize` / `cutover-plan` / `cutover-apply` against the live account with an evidence file whose `account_mode` is `live` (refused unless `ALPACA_MODE=live`; the account flat and order-free) writes the live activation record and initializes `accounts/alpaca/<live_account_id>/`. Restart the data plane: the selector finds the record, `select_live_clerk_runtime` composes the `real_live` authority (real trade port, real-custody envelope, arming ledger, arming gate, halt seam). The verdict reads `LIVE account … — real-money authority installed, no instance armed`.
3. **Deploy on live (R6, R7):** Stop the shadow instances and flatten the live account by hand first (R3, R15). The panel offers `live`; the deploy mints a **new** instance, seals it on the live id, and launches it — the admitted decision and the receipt both say that every ENTER refuses until the instance is armed. Each decision clock produces a rejected `LIVE_ARMING_REQUIRED` receipt; nothing can be submitted.
4. **Arm (slice 6 + R7):** `manage_alpaca_arming plan` / `apply` against the new instance; the ceremony reads its live-sealed binding, records the instance's own shadow receipt if it has one, and arms either way. Within one sync tick the gate holds a snapshot with the instance `armed`.
5. **The first real order (R5):** on the next decision, `accept_enter` runs `require_admission` → `require_arming_admission` → `require_envelope_admission`, commits `ENTER_ACCEPTED`, and `submit_enter` hands the leg to the real trade port. The first real-money order exists.
6. **Lapse, disarm, seal change, envelope edit, mid-session disagreement (R2, R10):** the tick sees the instance is no longer `armed`, logs `live_verdict_transition_halt` once, the verdict names it, and every ENTER refuses under the instance's code; its EXITs keep managing the position. Re-arm, and the next tick admits again — or Stop and flatten through the Clerk (R17).

## Error handling

Every refusal is a named code. Selection failures return a typed `unavailable` runtime (`LIVE_MODE_DISAGREEMENT`, `LIVE_ENVELOPE_MISSING`, `LIVE_CONTROL_UNAUTHENTICATED`, `ACTIVATION_RECORD_INVALID`, `SQLITE_CLERK_STARTUP_FAILED`) and the data plane boots degraded, never crash-loops (#2014). ENTER refusals are `AdmissionBlockedError`s with the codes of R5 and reach the receipt and the panel through the existing `rejected(...)` path. An unreadable arming ledger is a refusal at admission (`LIVE_ARMING_LEDGER_INVALID`) and at Start and Resume (R6); it is logged at error level once per transition (the slice-6 dedup) and every running instance's ENTER refuses under that code until the ledger is restored. A mid-session mode disagreement refuses every ENTER as `LIVE_MODE_DISAGREEMENT` (R2). A `live` deploy request whose receipt copy or execution mode the view does not offer is refused by the existing `offered` check; a `KeyError` in `_RECEIPT_COPY` is a contract bug the copy-table test catches. No silent handlers.

## Residuals (disclosed)

- **One primary authority per process (R1).** A graduated account cannot run shadow-mode bots in the same process as its live bots; a secondary-authority seam (the shadow facade selected per `shadow:` binding beside the live primary, the way `sim:` facades are selected per instance) is the open question the owner is deciding (in this slice, or a follow-up). Since shadow is a mode and not a requirement, nothing about arming or trading waits on it.
- **Graduation has no reversal (R1).** A `LiveDeactivationRecord` mirroring disarm — one append, the closed direction — is the follow-up; `dev_reset` correctly refuses live.
- **The arming gate is a 15 s cache of local evidence (R5).** Exact at the ET-date boundary because status is derived at admission time, but an arming or a disarm reaches the gate one tick late; the ceremony's JSON says so.
- **The halt writes no desired state (R10).** Decision 8's `desired_state = PAUSED` is not written, because PAUSED is observe-only for EXIT and would strand a position; the halt is the refusal, the warning and the verdict. Owner question E1 below is where this is decided for good.
- **The live instance is a new instance (R6).** Its rehearsal's custody history, if there was one, stays in the shadow database; the arming record's `shadow_receipt_sha256` (when the instance itself holds a receipt) and the shadow receipt ledger are the durable link between the two.
- **Cold start on the live authority is the paper path's (R18).** No shadow-namespace scan; the cutover's flat-and-order-free evidence and `recover()` are the proof.
- **A refused live boot labels its compat surfaces "paper"** (`custody_world_or_paper` falls back to `real_paper` on `None`), exactly as a refused paper boot does today; the verdict, not the compat status, is where a refused live boot is loud. Unchanged; noted.
- **A human trading the live account mid-session** withdraws day P&L to unknown for the rest of the ET day (slice 5 R5), so every program ENTER refuses `LIVE_ENVELOPE_UNOBSERVED` until the next day — owner question E7.
