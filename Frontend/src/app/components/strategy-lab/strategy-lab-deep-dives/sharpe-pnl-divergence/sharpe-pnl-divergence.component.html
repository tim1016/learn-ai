How to read this

A highlighted ribbon means cumulative P&L had a positive {{ study().trend_window_sessions }}-session trend while rolling Sharpe had a negative trend. That can reveal decaying trade quality behind an equity curve that still looks healthy.

Sharpe is not the portfolio’s growth rate. It compares average return with volatility. This is an exploratory decay diagnostic, not a trading signal or proof that the strategy has stopped working; confirm it with walk-forward OOS evidence, drawdown, trade count, and regime analysis.
