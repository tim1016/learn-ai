How to read these measures

Net profit is the sum of net session results. A positive total can conceal concentrated gains and long losing stretches; inspect the monthly pattern and the equity path together.

Risk uses fixed starting capital: drawdown percent = 100 × maximum minute-close drawdown / starting capital. This differs from the ordinary backtest’s session-end percentage of its running peak.

Daily return = net session profit / fixed starting capital. Sharpe = √A × mean(r − rƒ) / sample standard deviation(r − rƒ), where A is the calendar-derived annual session count and rƒ is the frozen daily risk-free return. An unavailable Sharpe is not zero.

Completed traded sessions measure activity; they are not a count of independent bets. A rule passing its limit is an eligibility check, not a probability of future success.
